## 模板运用评价

### 用对的地方
- 四段骨架完整
- `a vital step would be for government to issue...`（would be for sb to do ✅）
- `mainly due to fact that`（the fact that 意识有了，缺 the）
- `Apart from that, it is also important for government to...`（⑫结构 ✅）
- 结尾句1前半 `In summary, although the challenges posed by... are complex` ✅

### 用砸的地方
- `[lr:word_choice]` `The main important concern would be...`：concern 第3次误用（应为 cause），would be 应为 can be
- `[cc:template_scramble]` `Another contributor key factor is`：模板词序打乱（应为 Another key contributor to this issue is）
- `[tr:underdeveloped_support]` ⑪ 证据句（Recent reports...）连续第二篇丢失
- `[cc:template_slot_confusion]` 结尾 ⑭ 填错：posed by 后应接话题，误填 both the causes and solutions
- `[ga:sentence_fragment]` `the approach that address...`：主句动词 ensures 被改成 that 从句，全句无谓语
- `[ga:passive_misuse]` `posed by that is base on...`：刚学的 posed by 被乱用到措施段
- `[lr:collocation]` `paving of away`：pave the way for 第二次串错
