## 老师标黄套句：能用哪些

那些黄句是 **Agree / Disagree** 的骨架，不是万能句。这篇是 Cause / Effect，整段搬会错位。

| 黄句功能 | 意见题 | 本篇原因-影响题 |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| This perspective / reasoning is based on the understanding / observation that... | 能用：接你的理由 | 偶尔能用：接一个原因的解释。不要接假研究 |
| This position is further supported by various studies... / Recent findings from trusted sources... | 少用。考官认得假数据模板 | 这篇已经用了 60%、trusted sources，TR 减分 |
| Additionally, ... another strong argument | 意见题第二论点 | 原因题改成 Another major cause is... |
| Therefore, the significant benefits ... too important to ignore | 优点/同意收段 | 本题不是 benefits，不能用 |
| Furthermore, thus sparking widespread public debate... | 音乐范文里这句本身不完整 | 不要学，不要用 |

音乐范文里被标黄的句子本身也有错：`belief`、`festival`、`Furthermore, thus sparking` 不成句。灵活使用不等于整句复制；复制会把错误一起带走。

本篇已经误用的模板痕迹：
- 开头和前几篇一模一样
- as shown by some reports / as shown by the fact that over 60%
- Recent reports trusted sources verify that
- 结尾 needing a comprehensive measure（方案题套话）

Band 6 意见题最多留 1 句：`This is based on the observation that + 你亲眼能解释的现象`。不要编百分比，不要写 trusted sources。

## 逐段分析

### 开头

> 原文："the topic of people costing the decreasing time at home"

- `[lr:word_choice]`: cost 不是 spend。people spending less time at home / the decline in time spent at home
- `[lr:collocation]`: costing the decreasing time 不成搭配
- `[tr:template_mismatch]`: In today's discussions, the topic of X has gained a lot of interest 已在加班、垃圾两篇用过。模板文容易锁在 6 以下

> 原文："This essay looks into this main causes and potential effects of issues."

- `[ga:article_missing]`: the main causes
- `[ga:subject_verb_agreement]`: this + causes 不一致
- `[lr:word_choice]`: of issues 空。就是 this issue 的原因和影响

### 原因段

> 原文："The main concern of cause would be identifeid as rising work pressure"

- `[lr:spelling]`: identifeid → identified；cites → cities；employes → employees
- `[lr:collocation]`: the main concern of cause 不自然。One major cause is...
- `[lr:word_choice]`: involution 是中文“内卷”直译，考官不一定懂。用 intense competition / long working hours

> 原文："in order to dominate position of industry"

- `[ga:article_missing]`: dominate a position / gain a dominant position in the industry

> 原文："Another key contributor is issue that colorful entertainment places, mainly due to fact that..."

- `[ga:article_missing]`: another key contributor is the issue / is the rise of...；the fact that
- `[ga:word_form]`: convient → convenient；citis → cities；serives → services；affortable → affordable
- `[cc:paragraph_mix]`: 第二个原因（娱乐场所）没写完，后两句又回到加班。一段里两线交叉
- `[lr:collocation]`: make interest entertainment services 不成句。entertainment has become cheaper and more accessible

> 原文："In that context, young people have to work hard... it lead that invoting too much time on home is difficlut"

- `[cc:unclear_reference]`: that context / it 不知道指加班还是购物
- `[ga:subject_verb_agreement]`: it leads
- `[lr:spelling]`: invoting → investing；difficlut → difficult
- `[lr:collocation]`: investing too much time on home。少待在家应是 spend little time at home / find it difficult to stay at home

加班这个原因能扣“为什么不在家”。娱乐购物也能扣。段尾必须落回“所以待在家里的时间变少”，不要再写 keep up with the team。

### 影响段

> 原文："rasing involution ... influence on next generation ... over 60% ... live alone ... losing next generation"

- `[tr:effect_mismatch]`: 题目问少待在家的影响。独居、社会失去下一代，是工作压力的远后果，中间缺“不在家 → 家庭陪伴少 / 生育意愿低”这一步。跨太大
- `[tr:fake_evidence]`: over 60% 是编的。考官当模板
- `[lr:spelling]`: rasing → rising；leal → leads；cites → cities
- `[ga:article_missing]`: the next generation；choose to live alone；over 60% of people

影响应直接写：家庭关系变弱、孩子缺少陪伴、休息不足。独居可以留，但必须接到“很少回家 / 很少待在家里”。

> 原文："it impacts unhealth life way amoug younsters, with consequences stem from impulsive buying"

- `[cc:unclear_reference]`: it 指 involution、独居还是少待在家
- `[ga:word_form]`: unhealth → unhealthy；amoug → among；younsters → youngsters
- `[ga:finite_verb]`: with consequences stemming from。with 后不能用 stem
- `[tr:effect_mismatch]`: 冲动消费、暴食是减压方式，不是“不在家”的直接结果。要写，先说因为很少在家休息，所以用购物和暴食来放松

> 原文："Recent reports trusted sources verify that"

- `[tr:fake_evidence]`: 对应老师黄句 Recent findings from trusted sources。意见题里已经别扭，原因-影响题更假
- `[ga:article_missing]`: reports from trusted sources。但建议整句删掉，改成你自己能解释的观察

### 结尾

> 原文："the situation concerning presents... needing a comprehensive measure"

- `[lr:collocation]`: the situation concerning 缺宾语
- `[tr:template_mismatch]`: 题没问 solutions。写 measure / address challenges 是方案题套话。改成重述两个原因和两个影响

## 改写对比（目标 6.5）

保持原结构：开头 + 两个原因（加班、外出娱乐）+ 两个影响 + 结尾。只改表达和扣题。

In many cities, people now **spend far less time at home** than before. **This essay examines the main causes of this trend and its effects.**
（costing / decreasing time 改成 spend less time；删套话 gained a lot of interest）

**One major cause is long working hours.** In first-tier cities such as Shanghai, companies often push employees to stay late **in order to stay competitive**. As a result, many young workers **return home only to sleep**. **Another cause is the growth of cheap entertainment outside the home.** Shopping malls and other facilities are convenient, so people **who want to relax after work go out rather than stay in**.
（involution 换成 long hours / stay competitive；第二原因写完就落回“不待在家”；不用 with is）

**These changes affect family life and health.** If adults are rarely at home, children and partners receive less daily support, and some young people **live alone and delay settling down**. **Spending little time at home also makes it harder to rest properly.** After a long day, some people unwind through **impulse buying or overeating**, which can harm their health.
（影响接到少待在家；删 60% 和 trusted sources；keep 独居和冲动消费，但补上因果链）

**In conclusion, heavy workloads and attractive outdoor entertainment leave people with less time at home, weakening family relationships and encouraging unhealthy ways of relaxing.**
（不写 comprehensive measure）

## 提分优先级

1. **TR（5.5→6.0）：** 影响段每句问自己：这是“少待在家”的结果，还是“工作压力”的结果？结尾不要写方案。删掉 60% 和 trusted sources。
2. **GRA/LR（5.0→6.0）：** 写完用上次那 3 项扫：冠词、主谓、`with` 后不要谓语。再扫一遍拼写：cities / employees / convenient / unhealthy / difficult。
3. **套句：** 意见题才能用 This is based on the observation that。原因-影响题用 One cause is... / This leads to...。不要再用 In today's discussions...
