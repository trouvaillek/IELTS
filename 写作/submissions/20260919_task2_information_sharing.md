## 逐段主要问题

### 开头

> sharing much information has gained sparkly debate

- `[lr:word_choice]`: 米勒是 sparked extensive interest / sparked much interest。sparkly = 闪闪发亮，不能修饰 debate。has sparked a lively debate
- `[tr:incomplete_scope]`: 题目三个领域，开头只剩 scientific research
- `[ga:subject_verb_agreement]`: sharing ... are benefit → is beneficial / brings benefits
- `[lr:collocation]`: I argue the former view → I align with / I support the former view。argue 后面通常接 that 从句

### 主体1

> enhance research efficient / researcher who have / compensate negative / University Standford / research numbers / sucessful / as mainly due to / extremly provety / the stance attract / people right

- `[ga:word_form]`: efficient → efficiency；people's rights
- `[ga:article_missing]`: researchers who have different backgrounds
- `[lr:spelling]`: Stanford；successful；extremely；poverty；still（结尾也有 sill）
- `[lr:word_choice]`: research numbers → researchers；team numbers → team members
- `[cc:linker_stack]`: as mainly due to 只留 mainly due to the fact that 或 because
- `[ga:subject_verb_agreement]`: the stance attracts；shared research is beneficial
- `[tr:fake_evidence]`: Stanford / Oxford 具体结论是编的。改成现实场景：不同实验室公开数据后少做重复实验

acknowledge 那句方向对（公开有助于穷国获得药物和农业成果），语法要拆开：latest research may help countries that face extreme poverty.

### 主体2

> The viewpoint based on / which make / only account for / the most of research / stem from open resource

- `[ga:finite_verb]`: This viewpoint **is** based on the observation that
- `[ga:subject_verb_agreement]`: data ... make → makes；data ... account → accounts
- `[ga:article_missing]`: a small proportion；most research（不要 the most of）
- `[lr:word_choice]`: open resource → open-source / publicly available research
- `[tr:fake_evidence]`: Oxford 那句删掉或改成：many civilian technologies later used in defence first appeared in open journals

However 后的限制逻辑能用：涉密只是一小部分，所以不能因安全风险就反对尽量公开。这和 former 立场一致。

### 结尾

cautiously 能用，更常见 carefully。sill → still。open research outcomes 可以。最好补半句：business secrets can be limited, but most scientific and academic knowledge should be shared. 把题目三个领域收进去。

## 改写对比（目标 6.5，模板不动）

In today's discussions, the topic of **whether information should be shared as freely as possible** has **sparked much debate**. Some people argue that **sharing information in science, business and academia is beneficial**. Others believe that important and valuable information **cannot** be shared freely. **I align with the former view.**

My preference for this view is supported by **the importance of open research**, particularly through the argument that sharing research data can **enhance research efficiency**. This viewpoint is based on the fact that **researchers from different backgrounds can cooperate and learn from each other's strengths**. For example, **when laboratories publish methods and data, other teams can avoid repeating the same experiments**. It is crucial to acknowledge that **shared research can also help poorer countries**, mainly due to the fact that **new findings on infectious disease or food production can be used where drugs and food are scarce**. As a result, **this stance attracts** strong support from people who care about **human rights** and technology development.

On the other hand, the opposing argument has some reasonable points, particularly the claim that excessive sharing may lead to safety risks. **This viewpoint is** based on the observation that **some financial, business and defence data are not suitable to share**, which **has a significant influence on** national security. However, **information linked to national security accounts for only a small proportion of the total**. For example, **most academic papers and teaching materials can be shared without threatening a country**. Therefore, the opposing argument is limited and only partly true.

In summary, after carefully analyzing both views, I am **still** convinced that we should open **as many research and academic outcomes as possible**, **while keeping a small amount of highly sensitive information restricted**.
