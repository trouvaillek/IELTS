# 题目

Some parents give their children everything they ask for or allow them to do whatever they want. Is this a good way to raise children? What consequences could this style of parenting have as children grow up?

# 学生作文

While I certainly think it is good for children to get some of the things that they ask for, I do not think it is a positive thing to give them everything. Parents who allow their children to have and do whatever they want are doing them a disservice in the long run because these children will have unrealistic expectations of the world.

Arguably, children should have some of the things they want because this will help them to lead a happy and interesting life. They need things like toys, video games, and comic books to occupy their minds and help their brains develop. Nevertheless, it is also important that children do not get everything they desire because they may wrongly believe this is how the world works. They will just become frustrated with life since they may feel entitled to things they cannot have.

For entitled people, difficulties in their future careers and family life are likely to occur. In the workplace, it is important that you are a productive employee. However, people who got everything they wanted as a child may not be adept at working hard, so they may not be good at problem-solving and working through difficult issues. And when raising their future families, there will probably be much friction when entitled people realize their spouse and children will not blindly give in to their demands. It will be a frustrating time indeed!

Therefore, it is safe to say that satisfying all the wants of children is disastrous. They will grow up wrongly believing that if they want something, they will likely get it. Such unrealistic thinking will make them feel disappointed and lost whenever things do not turn out as they want.

## 小幅优化版

While it is reasonable for children to receive some of the things they ask for, I do not think parents should give them everything or allow them to behave however they wish. Such permissive parenting may harm children in the long run because it can give them unrealistic expectations of the world.

Children should certainly have access to toys, games and books that make their lives enjoyable and help them develop. However, if every request is satisfied, they may come to believe that desire alone is enough to obtain what they want. This can make them impatient and easily frustrated when they later face rejection or failure.

These attitudes can create problems in both work and family life. In the workplace, adults who were overindulged as children may struggle to accept criticism, work hard or solve difficult problems. Similarly, when they later raise their own families, they may experience tension if their spouse or children refuse to give in to their demands. As a result, permissive parenting can produce adults who find ordinary responsibilities difficult to manage.

Therefore, meeting every demand children make is not a healthy way to raise them. It may seem loving at first, but it can leave children self-centred, disappointed and poorly prepared for adult life.
