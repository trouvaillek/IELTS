## 本篇主要错因

### 1. with 结构

原句：

> with incomes is high slightly than expenditures

问题：`with` 后不能直接接完整谓语 `is`；词序也不对。

推荐：

> with income slightly higher than expenditure

规则：

- `with + 名词 + 形容词`
- `with + 名词 + V-ing`
- 不写 `with + 名词 + is/are ...`

### 2. 主谓一致

原句：

> Percentage for Program Services also have significant position

问题：`percentage` 是单数，谓语应为 `has`；更自然的图表表达应直接用 `account for`。

推荐：

> Program services account for 95.8% of total expenditure.

### 3. account for 的形式

原句：

> with Fundraising account for 2.6%

问题：`with` 后若接动作，应使用 V-ing。

推荐：

> with fundraising accounting for 2.6%

### 4. similar 与 likely 混淆

原句：

> distribution is likely Revenue Sources

问题：`likely` 表示“很可能”，不能表示“相似”。

推荐：

> The distribution is similar to that of revenue sources.

### 5. dominate 的自然表达

原句：

> have domination position as surprising other categories too far

问题：表达不自然，影响理解。

推荐：

> Donated food and program services dominate revenue and expenditure respectively, far ahead of the other categories.

### 6. 冠词与百分号

- `with percentage for Donated Food` → `with the percentage for donated food`
- `86.6` → `86.6%`
- `Percentage for Program Services` → `The percentage for program services`，或更自然地直接写 `Program services account for ...`

## 6.5 参考改写

The pie charts compare the income sources and spending of a children's charity in the United States in a given year.

Overall, the figures for income and expenditure are similar, with income slightly higher than spending. Donated food and program services dominate revenue and expenditure respectively, far ahead of the other categories.

Regarding revenue, a considerable disparity is observed among the categories, with the percentage for donated food the highest, at 86.6%, followed by community contributions and program revenue at 10.4% and 2.2% respectively. The proportions for the rest of the categories cluster around 0.3%, with other income at 0.4%, investment income at 0.2% and government grants at 0.2%. Total revenue was $53,561,580.

Expenditure shows a similarly uneven pattern. Program services account for 95.8%. The remaining categories are under 3%, with fundraising accounting for 2.6% and management and general costs at 1.6%. Total spending was $53,224,896.
