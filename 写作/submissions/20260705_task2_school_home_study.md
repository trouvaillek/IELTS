# 题目

Some people say that school education is not useful and the essence of study can be acquired at home. Do you agree? Why or why not? Use reasons and specific examples to explain your answer.

# 学生作文

Some people claim that school education is useless and that the essence of study can be gained at home. This essay disagrees, because school offers things that home learning cannot.

On the one hand, it is true that some learning can take place at home, and this has clear practical value. This is because books and the internet make knowledge widely available and cheap. For example, a motivated person can learn languages, history or computer skills at home without ever entering a classroom, which saves time and money. Still, this is only one part of education. Therefore, home learning is genuinely useful and should not be ignored.

On the other hand, school education offers things that the home cannot replace. The reason is that schools provide social skills, structure and qualified teachers. For example, children learn teamwork, friendship, discipline and how to deal with different people, which self-study alone cannot give, and these shape who they become. Therefore, school remains essential, even though useful learning can also happen at home.

In conclusion, although useful learning can take place at home, school provides social skills, structure and teachers that self-study cannot replace, so it remains essential. Overall, both have value, but school cannot be fully replaced.
