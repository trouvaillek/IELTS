## 改写对比（目标 6.5）

保持原论点：食物便宜；促销逼人多买；媒体宣传；限制不合理折扣。

Nowadays, large amounts of food bought from shops and restaurants are thrown away. **This essay examines why this happens and what can be done to reduce it.**

**One major reason is that food has become relatively cheap, especially in developed countries.** Snacks in shops and ingredients in restaurants cost less because production and transport are more efficient. In cities such as Shanghai, food takes only a small share of income, **so many people feel little need to save it**. **Another cause is that supermarkets and restaurants often offer discounts that push customers to buy more than they can eat.** If people want the cheaper deal, they overbuy, and the leftovers go into the bin.

**To reduce this waste, social media can raise awareness of saving food.** If people realise that many others still live in extreme poverty and hunger, they are more likely to finish what they buy. **Governments could also limit oversized discounts in shops and restaurants**, so customers purchase only what they need. Food waste would then fall more naturally.

**In conclusion, cheap food and aggressive discounts lead people to throw food away, but public campaigns and tighter rules on promotions can reduce this problem.**
