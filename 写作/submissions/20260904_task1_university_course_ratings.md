# 题目

The table gives information about students’ opinions on different aspects of courses they attended in a university.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features and make comparisons where relevant.

Percentage of students rating aspects of their course as “very good”

| Aspect/course | Pre-course information | Teaching quality | Tutor support | Course content | Resources |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Business | 62% | 93% | 96% | 74% | 58% |
| Computing | 61% | 88% | 85% | 69% | 56% |
| Maths | 64% | 64% | 90% | 72% | 51% |

## 逐段分析

### 第一段

原文：The table presents proportion of students rating aspects of their course as satisfying on different courses in a university.

- [ga:article_missing] proportion 前缺少限定词；这里可用 the proportions 或 the percentages。
- [tr:imprecise_category] 原表是评价为 “very good” 的学生比例；satisfying 弱化了评价等级，不应当作完全同义替换。
- [lr:collocation] “on different courses in a university” 与前面的 their course 重复且笨重，改成 comparing three university courses 更清楚。

修改：The table compares the percentages of students who rated five aspects of three university courses as “very good”.

### 第二段

原文：Overall, a considerable disparity is observed by three courses, with satisfaction of business having highlighting performance.

- [lr:prep_collocation] “is observed by three courses” 把课程写成了观察者。若保留该结构，用 differences between the three courses；更简单是直接写 Business received the highest ratings。
- [tr:overgeneralisation] 全表笼统称 considerable disparity 不够精确：教学质量相差最多，课前信息只相差 3 个百分点。
- [lr:collocation] “satisfaction of business having highlighting performance” 搭配不成立。应写 Business received the highest ratings in most categories。

原文：Its most aspects record the highest figures.

- [ga:determiner_order] “Its most aspects” 应为 most of its aspects；本题更自然的是 Business had the highest figures in four of the five categories。
- [cc:unclear_reference] Its 的指代不够清楚，且本句与上一句重复。合并为一句即可。

原文：In contrast, Maths and Computing have average performance with Math recording the lowest in teaching and resources.

- [tr:vague_comparison] “average performance” 没有明确参照，缺乏表格依据。可删去，用具体最高或最低概括。
- [lr:word_choice] 表格反映学生评价；performance 容易混淆为课程或学生的客观表现。使用 ratings。
- [lr:inconsistent_naming] 表中课程名为 Maths，全文应保持一致，避免 Math/Maths 混用。
- 正确部分：Maths 的 teaching quality 和 resources 的确最低，分别为 64% 和 51%。

### 第三段

原文：For five aspects of courses, student’s satisfaction of Tutor support are the highest percentage, with business recording the highest around 96%, followed by maths reaching 90%.

- [ga:subject_verb_agreement] satisfaction 是不可数名词，不能搭配 are；而且“满意程度等于最高百分比”语义不顺，建议重写主语。
- [tr:inaccurate_comparison] 若声称 Tutor support 在三门课程中都是最高项，则不正确：Computing 的教学质量是 88%，高于其导师支持的 85%。可以说导师支持的评价普遍较高。
- [lr:unnecessary_approximation] 表中给出精确的 96%，写 at 96% 即可；around 96% 不是严重数据错误，但没有必要。
- 修改：Tutor support received high ratings, at 96% for Business, 90% for Maths and 85% for Computing.

原文：For aspect of Teaching quality, there is significant disparity in three courses.

- [ga:article_missing] aspect 和 disparity 前缺少冠词；可写 There was a significant difference in ratings for teaching quality。

原文：satisfaction of Business and computing document similar percentage around 90%.

- [lr:collocation] “satisfaction ... document” 不成立；document 意为记录、记载，满意程度不能作这个动作的主体。直接写 The figures for Business and Computing are 93% and 88%, respectively。

原文：In contrast, Maths only record 64%.

## 提分优先级

1. 先修准确性：逐一核对 highest/lowest/only/all/rank，检查是否存在例外；五个项目至少各有一句或一个合并概括。

3. 最后检查主语：satisfaction/percentage/the figure 用单数谓语；ratings/percentages/the figures 用复数谓语。句首大写、句末句号、冠词一起检查。
