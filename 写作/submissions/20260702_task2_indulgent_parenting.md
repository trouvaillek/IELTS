# 题目

Some parents give their children everything they ask for or allow them to do whatever they want. Is this a good way to raise children? What consequences could this style of parenting have as children grow up?

# 学生作文

Some parents give their children everything they ask for and let them do as they please. This essay will argue that this is not a good way to raise children and explain the consequences it can bring.

On the one hand, giving children everything they want can also create problems as they grow up. The reason is that too much indulgence weakens important qualities such as patience, limits and responsibility. For example, a child who always gets their own way may become selfish and struggle to handle disappointment or rules as an adult, which shows that the harm is not obvious at first. This becomes especially serious when it affects their future work and relationships. Therefore, total indulgence usually leads to serious long-term consequences.

the other hand, a better approach sets clear limits while still showing love. The reason is that children need guidance to learn right from wrong and to become independent. For example, saying "no" at times and explaining the reason helps children learn patience, respect and self-control, which prepares them far better for adult life. Therefore, love combined with firm but fair limits raises healthier and stronger children.

In conclusion, giving children everything they want is not a good way to raise them because it fails to teach limits, and it can make them selfish and unable to cope as adults. Overall, love combined with clear limits raises stronger children.
