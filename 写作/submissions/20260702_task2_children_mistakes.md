# 题目

Some people think children have the freedom to make mistakes, while other people believe that adults should prevent children from making mistakes. Discuss both sides and give your opinion.

# 学生作文

In today's competitive society, many adults are concerned about how to educate their children. Some people believe that parents should prevent children from making mistakes, while others argue that children need the freedom to learn from their own errors. I believe children should be allowed to make minor mistakes, but adults should step in when the risks are serious.

On the one hand, children should be allowed to make minor mistakes because these experiences can help them become more responsible and independent. When children face the consequences of small errors, they learn to reflect on their choices and adjust their behaviour. For example, students who forget to bring their homework or fail at a new hobby may realise that they need to be better prepared or practise more patiently. These lessons cannot be taught simply through adults' advice. Therefore, giving children room to make small mistakes is important for their personal growth.

On the other hand, children's freedom should have clear limits because some mistakes may lead to serious consequences. Not every experience is suitable for children to learn from directly. For instance, if children frequently visit pornographic or violent websites, their mental and physical health may be harmed. Such harm is too serious to be treated as a useful lesson. Therefore, parents should prevent children from taking dangerous risks while still allowing them to explore safe challenges.

Overall, parents should not fully control their children, because safe mistakes can help develop their practical abilities and sense of responsibility. However, adults also need to set clear boundaries to protect children from serious harm. In this way, children can grow in a healthy and responsible manner.
