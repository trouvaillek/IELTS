## 改写对比（目标约 6.5）

**开头**
In many cities today, **household and industrial waste is growing at an alarming rate**. This essay will examine **the main causes of this rise** and suggest **practical measures to cut the volume of rubbish**.

**原因段（保留你的两个方向，纠正焦点）**
One major cause is **rapid urban growth**. As more people move into large cities, **daily consumption rises**, so **more packaging, food waste and discarded goods** are produced. For example, when infrastructure expands for economic growth but **consumption habits remain wasteful**, the **total amount of refuse** continues to climb. **A second cause is mass production driven by consumer demand**. Manufacturers supply snacks, bottled drinks and toys on a huge scale, and **single-use packaging and short-lived products** quickly become rubbish scattered across urban areas.

**方案段（对准“减量”）**
To tackle this, governments should **not only improve disposal capacity but also reduce waste at source**. They could **introduce stricter packaging rules**, such as limits on unnecessary plastic, and **require producers to take responsibility for recycling**. At the same time, public campaigns—on social media and through community events—can encourage people to **refuse single-use items, sort recyclables, and buy fewer disposable products**. If regulations and daily habits change together, the **amount of rubbish generated** is more likely to fall.

**结尾**
In conclusion, rising urban waste is mainly driven by **city growth and high-volume production**. A combined approach of **source-reduction policies and public awareness** would be more effective than disposal alone.
