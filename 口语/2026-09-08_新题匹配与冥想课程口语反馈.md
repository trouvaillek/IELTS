### Travelling 示例
**Where would you like to travel again?**
I'd love to visit Qingdao again. I went there with my family last year, and we really enjoyed the fresh seafood and the beer festival. It was a great chance for us to spend time together and relax.

**Do you enjoy travelling by car?**
Yes, especially when I travel with my family. We once drove to Qingdao, and the journey took about six hours. It was quite long, but having our own car made it easier to get around.

**Do you like visiting natural places?**
Yes, definitely. I visited Zhangjiajie and was amazed by its unusual mountains and the sea of green trees. The peaceful surroundings helped me get away from the hustle and bustle of city life.

难忘经历可用泰山日出；旅行问题可用张家界迷路。青岛的同行者是家人，张家界迷路故事的同行者是女朋友，不混用。

### Rubbish and recycling 示例
**Do you know anyone who cares about the environment?**
Yes, my friend Lu Wen cares a lot about the environment. He sometimes joins activities to pick up litter in parks and encourages people around him to avoid wasting resources. I've learned a lot from him.

原文是朋友捡垃圾，不等于自己参加过。被问个人经历时按实际回答。
以下分类习惯为新增示例，符合实际才使用：
Yes, I try to separate plastic bottles and cardboard from other rubbish. I don't always know which bin to use, so I check the signs first.

### Tiredness 示例
**When do you usually feel tired?**
Usually when I have a tight schedule and don't get enough sleep. For example, when I was preparing for the CFA exam, I often felt mentally exhausted because I had so much to focus on.

**What do you do when you feel tired?**
If I have enough time, I take a short nap. If I just need a quick break, I do a few minutes of meditation. It helps me calm my mind and feel refreshed.

**Do you feel tired after travelling?**
Sometimes, especially after a long hike. When I went to Mount Tai to watch the sunrise, I felt tired and cold while waiting at the top. But the view made the whole experience worthwhile.

## 三、口癖与表达习惯反馈
内容主线完整：压力大 → 朋友推荐 → 起初无聊 → 后来有效 → 继续使用。
最突出的问题是反复启动句子和回头纠错，而非缺少内容。

### 1. 重复开头
例：I remember that I remembered that in that in that Friday。
同时想交代多个背景，导致反复重启。
改成：Last year, I was preparing for several exams, including IELTS.
考试英文不确定时先用 several exams，避免卡住。

### 2. 纠错时整句重来
例：I find I found it that will help me I feel I felt comfortable with it。
同时改时态和结构，听者难以抓住主干。
改成：After a while, I started to feel more comfortable with it.
练习原则：不改变意思的小错先继续；需要修正时只改局部，例如 I find—sorry, found—it helpful.

### 3. 执着寻找复杂句型
例：what's the impression ... what's the most impression ...
目标句：What impressed me most was how such a simple activity could help me calm down.
备用句：I was surprised that such a simple activity could help me calm down.
更短：It was simple, but it really helped me relax.
卡住时切换简单句，不反复搜索固定句型。

### 4. 连接词叠加
例：So because so because of that；So but when I ...
按逻辑选择一个连接词，也允许短暂停顿。
可熟练使用：
- At that time, I was under a lot of stress.
- At first, I found it boring.
- After a while, I started to enjoy it.

### 5. 结尾不断追加
总结后又加面试、比赛、推荐朋友、价格、五分钟等新内容，增加临场组织负担。
只保留一个具体习惯，例如：
I still meditate before important interviews because it helps me clear my mind.
放在最终总结前。
若转写中的比赛实际指飞盘，可说 a frisbee match；需核对原意。

## 五、适合现场表达的整理版
保留用户原意，缩短句子。方括号为尚未确认的信息；不直接朗读括号。

I'd like to talk about a meditation course I took last year. I took it [地点或平台].

At that time, I was preparing for several exams, including IELTS. I was under a lot of stress and often struggled with sleep. A friend of mine had already tried a meditation course and found it helpful, so he recommended it to me.

During the course, the teacher asked us to sit quietly and focus on our breathing. At first, I found it a bit boring. I felt like I was just sitting there doing nothing, and I even felt a little silly.

But after practising for a while, I started to feel more comfortable with it. I was surprised that such a simple activity could help me calm down. Afterwards, I felt more relaxed and could get my head straight.

Now I still meditate before important interviews. It only takes a few minutes and helps me clear my mind. The course helped me build a better routine, and that's why it left such a strong impression on me.

## 六、whenever 的用法
We followed the teacher's instructions and did some simple breathing exercises.
我们按照老师的指导，做了一些简单的呼吸练习。

Whenever my mind wandered, I tried to bring my attention back.
每次我走神时，我都会试着把注意力拉回来。

- whenever：此处等于 every time，每当／每次……时，强调反复发生。
- my mind wandered：我走神了，思绪飘到了其他事情上。
- bring my attention back：把注意力重新集中回来；此处指回到呼吸上。
- 换成 when 也可以，但 whenever 更突出“每次走神都这样做”。

更简单的同义句：
Every time I got distracted, I tried to focus on my breathing again.
每次分心时，我都会试着重新专注于呼吸。

## 七、下次录音练习
1. 一句只说一个意思，说完再补充。
2. 卡住时换简单句，不强行寻找复杂句型。
3. 小错先继续，需要改时只改局部；录完后集中纠错。
4. 重点练熟：follow the teacher's instructions / focus on my breathing / my mind wandered / bring my attention back。
5. 补实际上课地点，并确认另一考试名称与比赛名称。

目标：把已经会的表达一次说完。
