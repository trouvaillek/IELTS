### Travelling 示例
**Where would you like to travel again?**
I'd love to visit Qingdao again. I went there with my family last year, and we really enjoyed the fresh seafood and the beer festival. It was a great chance for us to spend time together and relax.

**Do you enjoy travelling by car?**
Yes, especially when I travel with my family. We once drove to Qingdao, and the journey took about six hours. It was quite long, but having our own car made it easier to get around.

**Do you like visiting natural places?**
Yes, definitely. I visited Zhangjiajie and was amazed by its unusual mountains and the sea of green trees. The peaceful surroundings helped me get away from the hustle and bustle of city life.

Yes, I try to separate plastic bottles and cardboard from other rubbish. I don't always know which bin to use, so I check the signs first.

### Tiredness 示例
**When do you usually feel tired?**
Usually when I have a tight schedule and don't get enough sleep. For example, when I was preparing for the CFA exam, I often felt mentally exhausted because I had so much to focus on.

**What do you do when you feel tired?**
If I have enough time, I take a short nap. If I just need a quick break, I do a few minutes of meditation. It helps me calm my mind and feel refreshed.

**Do you feel tired after travelling?**
Sometimes, especially after a long hike. When I went to Mount Tai to watch the sunrise, I felt tired and cold while waiting at the top. But the view made the whole experience worthwhile.

## 三、口癖与表达习惯反馈
内容主线完整：压力大 → 朋友推荐 → 起初无聊 → 后来有效 → 继续使用。
最突出的问题是反复启动句子和回头纠错，而非缺少内容。

### 1. 重复开头
例：I remember that I remembered that in that in that Friday。
同时想交代多个背景，导致反复重启。
改成：Last year, I was preparing for several exams, including IELTS.
考试英文不确定时先用 several exams，避免卡住。

### 2. 纠错时整句重来
例：I find I found it that will help me I feel I felt comfortable with it。
同时改时态和结构，听者难以抓住主干。
改成：After a while, I started to feel more comfortable with it.
练习原则：不改变意思的小错先继续；需要修正时只改局部，例如 I find—sorry, found—it helpful.

### 3. 执着寻找复杂句型
例：what's the impression ... what's the most impression ...
目标句：What impressed me most was how such a simple activity could help me calm down.
备用句：I was surprised that such a simple activity could help me calm down.
更短：It was simple, but it really helped me relax.
卡住时切换简单句，不反复搜索固定句型。

### 4. 连接词叠加
例：So because so because of that；So but when I ...
按逻辑选择一个连接词，也允许短暂停顿。
可熟练使用：
- At that time, I was under a lot of stress.
- At first, I found it boring.
- After a while, I started to enjoy it.

### 5. 结尾不断追加
总结后又加面试、比赛、推荐朋友、价格、五分钟等新内容，增加临场组织负担。
只保留一个具体习惯，例如：
I still meditate before important interviews because it helps me clear my mind.
放在最终总结前。
若转写中的比赛实际指飞盘，可说 a frisbee match；需核对原意。

## 五、适合现场表达的整理版
保留用户原意，缩短句子。方括号为尚未确认的信息；不直接朗读括号。

I'd like to talk about a meditation course I took last year. I took it [地点或平台].

At that time, I was preparing for several exams, including IELTS. I was under a lot of stress and often struggled with sleep. A friend of mine had already tried a meditation course and found it helpful, so he recommended it to me.

During the course, the teacher asked us to sit quietly and focus on our breathing. At first, I found it a bit boring. I felt like I was just sitting there doing nothing, and I even felt a little silly.

But after practising for a while, I started to feel more comfortable with it. I was surprised that such a simple activity could help me calm down. Afterwards, I felt more relaxed and could get my head straight.

Now I still meditate before important interviews. It only takes a few minutes and helps me clear my mind. The course helped me build a better routine, and that's why it left such a strong impression on me.

## 六、whenever 的用法
We followed the teacher's instructions and did some simple breathing exercises.
我们按照老师的指导，做了一些简单的呼吸练习。

Whenever my mind wandered, I tried to bring my attention back.
每次我走神时，我都会试着把注意力拉回来。

- whenever：此处等于 every time，每当／每次……时，强调反复发生。
- my mind wandered：我走神了，思绪飘到了其他事情上。
- bring my attention back：把注意力重新集中回来；此处指回到呼吸上。
- 换成 when 也可以，但 whenever 更突出“每次走神都这样做”。

更简单的同义句：
Every time I got distracted, I tried to focus on my breathing again.
每次分心时，我都会试着重新专注于呼吸。

3. 小错先继续，需要改时只改局部；录完后集中纠错。
4. 重点练熟：follow the teacher's instructions / focus on my breathing / my mind wandered / bring my attention back。

- So overall,...

### 2. 优缺点类
**适用：What are the advantages/disadvantages...?**

- There are several advantages.
- From an individual's point of view,...
- From a wider social perspective,...
- However, there is also a downside...
- So overall,...

### 3. 比较类
**适用：A or B? / Which is better...?**

- It depends on the purpose.
- A is better when...
- On the other hand, B is better when...
- For example,...
- Personally / Overall,...

### 4. 差异类
**适用：What are the differences...?**

- They are different in several ways.
- First, in terms of function...
- Second, in terms of appearance...
- Another difference is...
- So although they are all..., they can be very different.

### 5. 社会现象类
**适用：Why are cities / people / places different?**

- I think it mainly depends on several factors.
- One factor is...
- Another is...
- Culture / age / lifestyle can also play a role.
- So in general,...

# 九、高建筑 Part 3 扩展答案

## Q1. Are there many tall buildings in your country?

**架构：总结 → 大城市 → 原因 → 小城市对比 → 总结**

Yes, definitely. There are a huge number of tall buildings in China, especially in large cities such as Shanghai, Beijing and Shenzhen.

I think the main reason is that these cities have very large populations but limited land. So building upwards is a practical way to accommodate more people without using too much space.

Also, tall buildings are often used for different purposes. Some are residential apartments, while others are offices, hotels or shopping centres.

However, in smaller cities or rural areas, buildings are usually much lower because there is more available land and the population density is lower.

So overall, tall buildings are much more common in major cities than in smaller places.

**核心逻辑：** large population → limited land → build upwards

---

## Q2. What are the differences between tall buildings in your country?

**架构：总结 → function → design → facilities → 总结**

I think tall buildings can be quite different in terms of their function, design and age.

First of all, their functions are different. Some buildings are mainly residential, while others are used as offices, hotels or shopping centres.

Secondly, their appearance can be very different. Newer buildings usually have a more modern design, with large glass windows and brighter lighting, while older buildings may look simpler and more practical.

Another difference is the facilities inside. New apartment buildings may have gyms, underground parking and better security systems.

So even though they are all tall buildings, the experience of using or living in them can be very different.

**可复用母框架：** function → appearance → facilities

---

## Q3. Why are different places laid out and designed differently?

**架构：总结 → land → local needs → culture → example → conclusion**

I think the design of a place mainly depends on available land, local needs and sometimes culture or climate.

First, land is a major factor. In crowded cities where land is expensive, buildings are usually taller and closer together. In smaller towns, there is more space, so buildings can spread out.

Another factor is people's lifestyle. For example, a business district may need offices, underground transport and shopping centres, while a residential area needs schools, parks and supermarkets.

Culture can also influence design. Some places try to preserve traditional architecture, while modern cities may prefer glass buildings and high-rise apartments.

So in general, the way a place is designed reflects both practical needs and local characteristics.

**可复用母框架：** land + needs + culture

---

## Q4. What are the advantages of living in tall buildings?

**架构：总结 → 个人优势 → 城市优势 → 缺点对比 → 结论**

I think there are several advantages, both for individuals and for cities.

From an individual's point of view, people living on higher floors may have a better view and experience less street noise. Some modern apartment buildings also provide good security, gyms and other facilities.

From a city-planning perspective, tall buildings are also an efficient use of land because they can accommodate a large number of people in a relatively small area.

However, there are some disadvantages as well. Residents often depend heavily on lifts, and waiting for them during rush hour can be annoying.

So overall, tall buildings are efficient and convenient, but whether people enjoy living in them depends on their personal preferences.

**可复用视角：** individual level → city level

---

## Q5. Why do some people like to remodel and decorate their homes themselves?

**架构：总结 → personal control → money → emotional value → example**

I think there are mainly three reasons.

The first is personal preference. If people decorate their own homes, they can choose the colours, furniture and style they really like instead of following someone else's ideas.

The second reason is cost. Hiring professional designers can be expensive, so doing some of the work themselves may help them save money.

Another reason is that people may feel more emotionally connected to a home they designed themselves. They may feel proud when they see the final result.

For example, someone might paint the walls or choose furniture by themselves, and even if the result isn't perfect, it can still feel more personal.

So I think DIY decoration gives people more control and a stronger sense of achievement.

**可复用素材：** personal preference / save money / sense of achievement

---

## Q6. What kind of interior design style do most people like?

**架构：总结 → young people → reasons → older people → depends**

I think many people, especially young adults, prefer a simple and modern interior style.

One reason is that apartments in large cities are often not very spacious. If there is too much furniture or decoration, the room can feel crowded. A simple design can make the space look cleaner and larger.

Another reason is convenience. Modern furniture is often easier to clean and maintain, which suits people with busy lifestyles.

However, older people may prefer a more traditional style because it feels warmer or more familiar to them.

So I think interior design really depends on age, lifestyle and personal taste, but simple modern styles are probably the most common among younger people.

**高频收尾句：** It really depends on age, lifestyle and personal taste.

# 十、Course / Memory Part 3 扩展答案

## Q1. What do people need to remember in their daily lives?

**架构：总结 → work/study → personal life → technology → conclusion**

I think people need to remember a lot of things in daily life, especially things related to work, study and personal responsibilities.

For example, at work, people need to remember deadlines, meetings and important tasks. Students also need to remember exam dates, assignments and what they have studied.

In personal life, people may need to remember birthdays, appointments and things they need to buy.

Nowadays, because people have a lot on their plate, it's difficult to remember everything, so many people rely on smartphones, calendars and reminder apps.

So I think modern life requires people to remember more information, but technology also makes it easier to manage.

---

## Q2. What can we learn from history?

**架构：总结 → mistakes → patterns → society → example → conclusion**

I think history is useful because it helps us understand both past mistakes and long-term patterns in society.

One important thing we can learn is that many problems have happened before. For example, wars, economic crises and political conflicts often have causes that develop over many years.

By studying these events, governments and individuals may be able to make better decisions and avoid repeating the same mistakes.

History can also help us understand why different countries have different cultures, political systems and attitudes today.

For example, studying past economic crises can help people understand why governments sometimes change interest rates or financial policies.

So I don't think history is only about remembering dates. It's more about understanding cause and effect.

**高频观点句：** History is not only about remembering dates. It's about understanding cause and effect.

---

## Q3. Why do some people have a better memory than others?

**架构：总结 → natural ability → lifestyle → technique → example**

I think it is a combination of natural ability and daily habits.

Some people may naturally have a stronger memory, just like some people are naturally better at languages or mathematics.

However, lifestyle is also very important. If someone sleeps well, exercises regularly and is less stressed, they may find it easier to concentrate and remember information.

Learning methods also make a difference. For example, reviewing information repeatedly or connecting new ideas with familiar things can make them easier to remember.

So I don't think memory is completely fixed. People can improve it through better habits and effective learning methods.

**高频观点：** memory is not completely fixed

---

## Q4. Do people like things of memorial significance?

**架构：总结 → emotional value → memory trigger → family → digital age**

Yes, I think most people do, because these objects often have emotional value rather than financial value.

For example, an old photo, a gift from a family member or a ticket from a memorable trip may not be expensive, but it can remind someone of an important period in their life.

These objects can also help people preserve memories that might otherwise become less clear over time.

I think this is especially true for things connected with family, childhood or important relationships.

Even today, when people store thousands of photos on their phones, physical objects can still feel more personal.

So the value of these things usually comes from the memories attached to them.

**重点表达：** emotional value rather than financial value / the memories attached to them

---

## Q5. Which can help people remember things better, words or photos?

**架构：总结 → photos advantage → words advantage → depends on task → conclusion**

I think it depends on what people are trying to remember, but for everyday memories, photos may be more effective.

Photos provide visual information, so they can quickly remind people of a place, a person or an experience. Sometimes one photo can bring back a whole memory.

However, words are better for detailed or abstract information. For example, if you are studying history or preparing for an exam, written notes may help you remember facts and explanations more clearly.

Personally, I think the best way is to combine both. For example, diagrams with short notes can make information easier to understand and remember.

So photos are better for visual memories, while words are better for detailed information.

**A/B 比较母框架：** It depends on the purpose → A is better for... → B is better for... → combine both

---

## Q6. Can technology help people remember things better? How?

**架构：总结 → reminders → storage → learning → downside → conclusion**

Yes, definitely. I think technology can help people remember things in several ways.

First, smartphones can remind us about meetings, birthdays, deadlines and other important tasks, so we don't need to keep everything in our heads.

Second, technology can store information for us. For example, people can use notes, photos or cloud storage to save information and check it later.

It can also help with learning. Some apps use flashcards or repeated review to help people remember vocabulary or other information.

However, there is also a downside. If people rely too much on technology, they may stop trying to remember things themselves.

So I think technology is very useful as a support tool, but people shouldn't depend on it completely.
