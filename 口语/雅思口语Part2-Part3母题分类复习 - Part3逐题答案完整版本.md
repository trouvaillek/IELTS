### 🧱 1. 高频回答架构
+ `For this topic, I'd like to talk about...`：Part2 开头，直接进入人物/地点/经历。
+ `It dates back to a couple of months/years ago...`：交代时间背景。
+ `I was first introduced to it/him through...`：说明第一次接触某人、某物、某地方。
+ `The main reason is... / Apart from that... / At the same time...`：把一个观点自然说长。
+ `Compared with A, B is more...`：做年龄、城市、线上线下、过去现在对比。
+ `From my perspective / As far as I am concerned...`：表达个人观点。
+ `It really depends on the situation.`：不想绝对回答时的缓冲句。
+ `There are a wide range of... / all kinds of...`：列举前的万能铺垫。
