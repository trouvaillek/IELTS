# 雅思口语 Part1 母题分类复习 - 素材丰富版本
> **定位：** 这份文档把 Part1 做成“轻量母题 + 逐题答案 + 快速套句”。重点不是背很长，而是每个问题都能立刻联想到你自己的高频素材。
>

## 📖 使用方法
+ **第一步：** 先看 `Part1 通用回答素材库`，把高频句型练顺。
+ **第二步：** 看 `Part1 轻量母题分类`，知道每个问题能借用哪些相邻话题。
+ **第三步：** 刷每个 `Txx-Qx`，直接看它下面的 `可归类 / 可借用话题`、`可套素材`、`参考回答`。
+ **口语原则：** Part1 答案控制在 2-4 句，保持自然、稳定、能现场说出来。

## 🧰 Part1 通用回答素材库
### 🧱 高频回答架构
**1. 喜不喜欢类**

+ `Yes, I’m really into...`
+ `Not really, it’s not my cup of tea.`
+ `It depends on my mood / schedule / budget.`

**2. 频率类**

+ `I do it from time to time.`
+ `I rarely do it because I have a lot on my plate.`
+ `I usually do it when I want to relax.`

**3. 过去现在对比类**

+ `I used to..., but now...`
+ `When I was a kid..., but these days...`
+ `In the past, I was more likely to..., but now I tend to...`

**4. 重要性/评分类**

+ `On a scale from 1 to 10, I’d like to give it a 10.`
+ `It plays an important role in my daily life.`
+ `I can’t imagine my life without it.`

**5. 优缺点类**

+ `The biggest benefit is that...`
+ `The downside is that...`
+ `It can save time / relieve stress / broaden my horizons.`

**6. 个人例子类**

+ `For example, I often...`
+ `A good example would be...`
+ `I remember once...`
+ `In most cases, I would...`

**7. 选择/比较类**

+ `Compared with A, B is more practical for me.`
+ `A is more convenient, while B feels more personal.`
+ `If I have to choose, I would go for...`

**8. 不确定/看情况类**

+ `Well, it really depends.`
+ `It depends on the type of things.`
+ `It depends on whether I finish my work.`

| **忙碌/压力** | `I have a lot on my plate.`; `I suffer from a super tight work schedule.`; `I have to beat the clock.`; `My daily life can be described as a hectic routine.`; `I need to catch up on my work.` |
| **放松/开心** | `It helps me let go pressure.`; `It helps me relieve stress.`; `It is pretty pressure-releasing.`; `It helps me recharge my battery.`; `It can cheer me up.`; `I can regain inner peace.` |
| **学习/效率** | `It can enhance my work efficiency.`; `It helps me prioritize my daily tasks.`; `It helps me stay organized.`; `It can split tough tasks into small pieces.`; `It helps me broaden my horizons.` |
| **社交/朋友/家人** | `It can enhance friendship.`; `It can improve our relationship.`; `spend quality time with my family`; `express sincere care and affection`; `true confidant / life-confidant`; `put myself in their shoes` |
| **城市/环境** | `first-tier city`; `fast-paced life`; `well-developed subway network`; `convenient public facilities`; `heavy traffic jams`; `high living costs`; `run away from hustle city life` |

| **评价/比较** | `Well, it depends.`; `It depends on the situation.`; `On a scale from 1 to 10, I’d like to give it a 10.`; `The main reason is that...`; `Apart from that...`; `The downside is that...` |


### 🧩 一分钟 Part1 组合公式
+ **喜欢题：** `Yes, I’m really into...` + `because it helps me...` + 个人例子。
+ **不喜欢题：** `Not really, ... is not my cup of tea` + 原因 + `I prefer...`。
+ **看情况题：** `Well, it depends on...` + A 情况 + B 情况。
+ **过去现在题：** `I used to..., but now...` + 原因变化。
+ **城市/地点题：** 先说 `convenient public facilities`，再补 `traffic jams / fast-paced life`。
+ **休闲/爱好题：** 先说 `let go pressure`，再补 `have fun / recharge my battery`。

+ `save time / save money`

+ `waste time and energy`
